Evo-DexBench Panda + Shadow visualization

Robot assembly, joint transforms, joint limits, and visual references are
exported from dex_benchmark/robots/assets/panda_shadow/
panda_shadow_distributed_tactile_123.urdf in the supplied Evo-DexBench repository.
The default pose comes from robots/panda_shadow/panda_shadow.py.
Manifest.json records source-relative paths and SHA256 values for each asset.
The visual GLB files are copied without changes.

The website renders URDF kinematics and actual visual meshes. The comparison
scene and opening/closing motion illustrate the protocol; they do not execute
the physics engine and are not evaluation rollouts.
